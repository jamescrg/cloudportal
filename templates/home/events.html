{% comment %} The home page's calendar card: a one-week slice of the calendar's month view, Sunday to Saturday, drawn by FullCalendar from home-week-feed (apps/home/agenda.py) through the calendar's own component (events-calendar.js). It works as the month view does: the weekday header row over the days, today's number circled, an all-day event a filled chip and a timed one a dot, its time and its title. An event or a task drags to another day; a click opens it; a click on an empty part of a day adds an event on that day; a day's number opens the calendar on it. Where the home page's setting has them, the user's open tasks are on their days, a box before the title that marks the task done; on the week holding today the overdue ones are gathered at the top of today, dated and in the urgent colour. The chevrons step a week; on a phone the week is its agenda. The grid refetches when an event or a task changes. {% endcomment %}
