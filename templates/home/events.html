{% comment %} A week, Sunday to Saturday, as a strip of columns: a row of the calendar's month view, today's column marked. Each column heads with the weekday and, in its upper right, the day of the month (the month is in the stepper), and opens the calendar on it. Under it the day's entries, drawn as the month view draws them: an all-day event a filled chip, a timed one a dot, its time and its title. Where the home page's setting has them, open tasks are on their days as the month view has them, a box before the title: the box marks the task done and the rest of the chip opens it. On this week, tasks overdue from before it are gathered at the top of today, dated and in the urgent colour. A day with nothing on it keeps its column, blank (a free day is worth seeing); on a phone the strip stacks and empty days drop out. The chevrons swap in the week before or after (views.week), and the card redraws itself when a task changes. {% endcomment %}
